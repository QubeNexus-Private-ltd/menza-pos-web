Step 1 · Scan

A table. A QR code.

A guest sits down and scans the QR code on the table. The menu is right there on their phone.

Step 2 · Order · Menza POS

They order. It travels.

The guest places the order directly from the table. Menza POS manages orders, tables, billing, payments and restaurant operations from a centralized application, and the request moves on to the kitchen.

Step 3 · Prepare · Menza KDS

The chef sees it instantly.

The ticket appears on Menza KDS, a digital Kitchen Display System for the kitchen team.

  • Manage incoming orders
  • Monitor preparation timers
  • Prioritize urgent tickets
  • Track ready orders

Step 4 · Serve · MenzaServe

Ready. Deliver it.

MenzaServe helps restaurant staff coordinate service workflows, monitor active orders and track order progress, so the finished dish reaches the right table.

Step 5 · Enjoy

Enjoyed. Rated. Five stars.

The guest enjoys the meal and rates the restaurant. One connected flow, from the first scan to the last smile.
